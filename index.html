SLA Breach Predictor

AI that forecasts SLA breaches before they happen — scoring risk by ticket, contract, site and technician, and recommending the mitigation that protects your margin and your client relationship.

Why SLA breaches are predictable

Most SLA penalties are not surprises — the signals were in the ticket queue days earlier: rising backlog age, parts in transit, technician capacity gaps, repeat-visit patterns. SLA Breach Predictor reads those signals continuously and turns them into an early-warning radar for your service operation.

What it does

Risk scoring per ticket

Every open ticket gets a live breach-probability score based on SLA clock, priority, parts status and history of similar tickets.

Contract-level exposure

See penalty exposure per contract and per client, with trends by week, so account managers can act before renewal conversations get difficult.

Mitigation recommendations

Suggests the cheapest action that removes the breach: reassign a technician, escalate parts logistics, or renegotiate a window with the client — in writing.

Alerts where you work

Email and webhook alerts when a ticket crosses your risk threshold, with the full context attached.
